# Back Office Page Pattern Map

The Dashboard is the Golden Master; every other page inherits its shell, typography, color discipline, radii, depth, and curve language.

## Family Tree
Use the Golden shell. Give the tree canvas the largest surface on the page. Use the supplied Golden tree artwork only as decorative/brand illustration; the operational genealogy graph should remain interactive and data-driven. Curved burgundy header, cream workspace, burgundy detail drawer.

## Members
Curved page header + member count/household metrics. Use cards/table on warm paper, photo avatars, gold active filters. Do not turn it into a flat bootstrap table.

## Events
Reuse the exact event-hero curve language. Featured event may use photography. Secondary events use paper cards with date block, RSVP/status and location.

## Dues & Payments
Use At-a-Glance metric style for collected/outstanding/participation. Keep financial tables rectangular and highly scannable; curves belong in header and surrounding panels, not table rows.

## Photos & Memories
Photography-forward page. Burgundy curve header, album chips, warm paper gallery, rounded image crops.

## Documents
Restrained version of the design system: cream canvas, burgundy title, paper folders/cards, gold/olive status accents.

## Messages
Warm two-pane messaging workspace on paper. Burgundy active conversation, cream thread, subtle curved top header only.

## Settings
Minimal decoration. Keep typography and color system, but prioritize clear forms and safe destructive actions.
